# Primary-Text Engine Standard (Meta) — v2

Structural standard for Meta **primary text**, evidence-based (2025/26). Replaces run-on "chunk" copy. Applies to all multi-text ad copy.

## Why (research-backed)
- 90%+ impressions are mobile; ~**80% of users never click "See more."** → hook must land in the **first ~125 characters**, and copy must be **scannable** (short lines, white space, one idea per line). Walls of text bury the hook = dead ad.
- Emoji lift CTR broadly **but** are flagged as risky "especially in legal/finance/health," can look unprofessional / trigger ad review, and older audiences skew non-emoji. → **functional emoji only, sparse, testable — never blanket.**

## Format (hard rules)
```
[HOOK ≤125c — pain callout / question, 0–1 subtle emoji, front-loaded]

[agitate — one short line, NO emoji]
[reframe/solution — one short line]

✅ [bullet]  ✅ [bullet]   ← emoji-anchored scan block (optional)

[CTA line] 👉
Attorney Advertising.          ← compliance line, ALWAYS clean (no emoji adjacent)
```
- One idea per line; blank line between beats (PAS: Problem → Agitate → Solution).
- Reinforce the CTA in text even though the button exists.

## Emoji policy — legal/injury vertical
- **Allowlist (functional only):** ✅ ⚠️ 👉 📋 ⚖️ 📍 ⏳
- **Banned:** 🎉 🔥 💰 💵 🤑 😍 🚨(alarmist) and any celebratory/money/outcome-implying emoji (money emoji reads as a guaranteed-payout claim — compliance risk).
- **Cap ~2–3 per ad.** Use as bullet anchors / hook accent only.
- **Never** on the somber/agitation line (someone got hurt) and **never** adjacent to "Attorney Advertising."
- **Test it:** ship emoji vs no-emoji as a copy variable; older + legal audiences can go either way.
